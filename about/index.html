About

I usually start by asking how the pieces fit together.

I'm a software engineer working mostly on backend services, integrations, and automation. A lot of my work involves changes that cross several services, components, or teams.

About

I rarely look at a task in isolation. I usually want to understand what depends on it, who owns the surrounding pieces, and what happens the next time the same problem comes back.

I consider documentation part of the work. Tests, runbooks, diagrams, and clear explanations should help the next person make a safe change without having to find the one person who remembers everything.

That carries into teaching and collaboration. I like explaining a difficult technical problem at a whiteboard, drawing the relationships between the parts, and working through the trade-offs with the people involved.

Principle

Separate what changes from what stays the same

Make the differences that affect behavior visible instead of burying them in special cases.

Principle

Make the next change easier

Use tests, automation, and documented steps so recurring work does not need to be rediscovered.

Principle

Keep ownership clear

A good abstraction should make it clearer who owns what and where a decision belongs.
