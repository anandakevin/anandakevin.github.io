01 / Scope

Context and ownership.

Ledger systems and data pipelines

Several years on a General Ledger team in a major Indonesian bank — journal workflows, year-end procedures, legacy COBOL-era processing, and the ETL-shaped work that keeps accounting data trustworthy at scale.

Focus

  • Correctness under pressure — batch and simulation cycles where a missed edge case shows up in regulatory reporting, not in a unit test log.
  • Legacy-to-maintainable — moving or improving brittle processing so the next engineer can understand and change it.
  • Operational support — runbooks, internal tooling, and dashboards that help people respond when production is already noisy.

This work was less about fashionable tools than keeping accounting data correct: understanding data movement, following careful procedures, and untangling processes that had outlived several generations of developers.
