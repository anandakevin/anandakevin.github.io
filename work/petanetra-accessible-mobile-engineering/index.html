Accessible mobile engineering

Volunteer software engineering for Petanetra — an accessibility-oriented product that combines on-device object detection with offline, multilingual spatial guidance.

Focus

  • On-device ML integration — ONNX and TFLite inside a maintainable Android architecture rather than a one-off demo.
  • Instrumentation — attribution and navigation tracking refined enough to learn where users struggle without guessing from anecdotes.
  • Team coordination — informal technical leadership across mobile and platform work when the roadmap needed slicing, not just tickets.

This work applies some of the same habits to mobile and applied ML: deal with offline constraints, keep the app maintainable, and use product feedback from real users.
