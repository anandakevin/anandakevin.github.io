Selected as 1 of 38 trainers for a national Digdaya hackathon program across two connected tracks: Essential-track Design Thinking and Practitioner-track Startup Business Development. Four full-day sessions moved from user-centered problem framing into MVP, business modelling, execution readiness, and demo storytelling.

Essential Track: Design Thinking

  • Two completed full-day online sessions in May 2026.
  • Problem framing, value proposition, Business Model Canvas, SMART goals, MVP/prototype/testing, and impact metrics — taught as one continuous arc before teams build.
  • Official trainer performance ratings: 4.77 and 4.85 out of 5.00 (4.81 average) across 43 qualitative feedback rows. Participants highlighted customer-first framing, measurable goals, structured delivery, practical examples, and responsive support.

Participant feedback

Participants most often highlighted the structured progression from customer problems and value propositions to measurable goals and project decisions. They described the material as clear, practical, and easy to follow, with concrete examples and responsive support connected to their hackathon work.

Practitioner Track: Startup Business Development

  • Two completed full-day sessions on 2026-07-04 in Jakarta and 2026-07-05 online.
  • Full-material delivery covered MVP Planning and Product-Market Fit, Business and ROI Modeling for Innovation, Startup and Team Execution Readiness, and Demo Storytelling and Video Pitch Production.
  • Participant feedback repeatedly focused on user persona and problem validation, MVP scope, ROI and business modelling, team roles and milestones, pitch structure, and demo-video production. The sessions included hands-on work, breakout-room support, and discussion.
  • Official trainer performance ratings: 4.87 and 4.71 out of 5.00 (4.79 average). Session ratings were 4.84 and 4.68 out of 5.00.

Participant feedback

Feedback on the Practitioner sessions most often mentioned MVP scoping, business modelling, execution planning, and turning the final idea into a pitch and demo. Feedback also frequently mentioned the interactive format, practical examples, and responsiveness to questions.
