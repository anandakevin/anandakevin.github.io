How I mentor project teams under deadline pressure

  • Tighten scope until success criteria are testable before the demo deadline.
  • Align frontend, backend, data, ML, and device boundaries so integration surprises show up early.
  • Match depth to team maturity — some groups need architecture sketches; others need permission to delete features.
