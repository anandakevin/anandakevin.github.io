Selected as 1 of 8 facilitators for a national back-end and DevOps academy cohort, mentoring 25 national scholarship awardees through self-paced modules and live biweekly check-ins.

Rhythm

  • Baseline the group in the first session: who is new to the stack, who is already teaching the room about orchestration.
  • Each follow-up: review progress and blockers, reflect on what changed, and set realistic two-week goals rather than treating the dashboard as the whole mentoring relationship.
  • Usually 10–15 mentees joined each live session, so the rhythm had to support both complete newcomers and participants already understanding about Kubernetes.
  • Leave space for open topics — career paths, frontend/backend contracts, containerization, stack integration, and working internationally.

Outcomes (pattern level)

Cohort completion landed around 60% average module completion across a group balancing the program with other commitments. The durable win is steadier progress and clearer mental models of how backend pieces connect, not a single perfect score week.
