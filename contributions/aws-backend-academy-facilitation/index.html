Selected as 1 of 8 facilitators for AWS Back-End Academy, supporting 25 national scholarship awardees through self-paced learning and biweekly live sessions.

Facilitation

I ran 4 one-hour sessions, usually attended by 10–15 participants at a time.

The sessions covered:

  • backend development
  • containers and Docker
  • Kubernetes
  • deployment workflows
  • frontend/backend integration
  • career development and working internationally

The cohort had a wide range of experience levels, from participants still building their backend foundations to others already comfortable with Kubernetes and deployment tooling.

Each session combined progress check-ins, technical discussion, blocker review, and open Q&A rather than following a fixed lecture format.
