Contact

Email is the best default when you want a substantive back-and-forth about engineering, collaboration, teaching, speaking, or an opportunity. LinkedIn is the shorter professional timeline; GitHub and Kaggle hold public code and notebooks if you prefer to orient from work first.

I’m especially interested in backend and platform work, integrations, automation, and teaching or mentoring where clear explanation is part of the deliverable. A short note on what you’re building, the constraints, and whether you prefer async email or a short call helps me respond usefully.
