Cloud and machine-learning programs

Substantial AWS and machine-learning programs that extended the formal computer-science degree and certification path.

Two substantial learning programs from the historical portfolio inventory:

  • AWS re/Start — completed in 2023; the Credly graduate badge was issued on April 24, 2023. The full-time, classroom-based program covered IT fundamentals, Linux, scripting, core AWS services, and professional skills.

Scholarship-supported machine-learning training covering applied work across natural-language processing, time series, and image classification, with additional study of reinforcement learning, recommendation systems, and deployment architecture.
